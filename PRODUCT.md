# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React (user's choice). Homepage only in the first pass.

## Users

- Manufacturers and applied-systems / HVAC enterprises evaluating a platform to connect field sales, reps, ERP, IoT and digital twins.
- Manufacturers' reps, contractors and enterprise IT leaders looking for cloud CRM, ERP, bid, data-lake and IoT tools.
- Companies and entrepreneurs exploring Ciright's Technology Equity Partnership ("Dream it, Build it").
- Security and identity buyers interested in Keyra (trust layer) and CyberONE / ONEPass passwordless cards.

## Product Purpose

Ciright helps companies thrive by integrating connected-enterprise and mobile solutions that raise the efficiency of the whole business ecosystem while accelerating growth and reducing costs. The homepage must make Ciright read as a modern AI, spatial-computing and digital-transformation company and route visitors to a demo / contact.

## Positioning

- Ciright Core: "the connection engine of the digital enterprise" — deployable in cloud, private cloud, hybrid or on-premises, with 3,000+ published APIs enabling department-by-department transformation.
- Inception Twin: the digital twin is conceived before the physical machine (spatial computing + Ciright IoT).
- Applied Systems platform built exclusively for the applied systems / HVAC industry, integrating selection software, order-entry ERP, digital twin and IoT.
- 25+ years of technology leadership (mobile computing to spatial computing and AI). Philadelphia roots.

## Operating Context

Enterprise B2B evaluation: visitors compare platforms, look for proof (case studies, integrations), and book a call with sales. Existing login at myciright.com; contact at contactus.ciright.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities (from ciright.com): Ciright Core (Cloud Manager, Users, ERP, CRM, Contracts, Work), Enterprise API Switch / API Broker, Spatial Computing (AR/VR/MR, digital twins), Applied Systems, IoT software (Machine Monitor, C-Stat) and sensors (IoT Bridgeboard, Environment Sensor), Fintech engines (Canopy, KYC, P2P payments, loyalty, affiliate, crypto key), Radar AgenticVR, Ciright Live, Secure Access (CyberONE card, ONEPass), Partnership program.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ciright. Tagline usage on site: "See Right".
- User-pinned visual reference: the new UI should follow the language of cuberto.com (studio-grade, motion-led). The old ciright.com look is to be replaced.

## Evidence on Hand

- Stats shown on ciright.com: 81 countries, 4B users, 305 customers, 58,317 cups of coffee.
- Case study: Daikin Applied Systems — forecast horizon extended from 30 days to 9–12 months with Ciright Monolithic Core (TriState HVAC office integration).
- Brands/portfolio: Keyra ("The Trust Layer of the Internet — Verifying Humans. Authenticating Agents. Protecting Every Transaction."), Centili, Sansar (Lost Horizon festival, 4.36M attendees), CyberONE (Microsoft certified FIDO2 partner), ONEPass, Events, Works, Source Digital.
- CEO quote: Joe Callahan — "When you have passion, vision, drive and inspiration, you have the foundation for a team that's destined to win… Add in an engaging environment and a lifetime of experiences with some Philadelphia grit and you have Ciright."
- No client testimonials, customer logos (beyond Daikin in the case study), awards or pricing exist; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Prove the platform with real specifics (APIs, deployment models, the Daikin result) rather than adjectives.
2. One clear action everywhere: talk to Ciright / schedule a demo.
3. Enterprise credibility first; spectacle serves comprehension.
4. Keep product families legible: Core, Spatial, Applied, Secure Access, Partnership.
