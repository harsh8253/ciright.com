# Design System: Ciright

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Night Shift Over White Paper"**

Ciright reads as a studio-grade editorial page that keeps dropping into night. The ground is near-white paper set in one grotesk, and the product lives in deep night-indigo fields that rise out of it: stacked solution cards, a trust band, a black brands capsule, and a black closing block. Light pages carry argument (statements, component index, case study, CEO quote); night fields carry the product and the proof. The alternation is the rhythm of the whole page. The Products page runs in the same world: its catalogue is six open spreads on paper, one per product family, each listing its products as named type. The Brands page runs in it too: eight brands stand as tall full-bleed photo posters in one reel on paper, navy monochrome at rest, which the page walks sideways past a single lit focus point as the visitor scrolls down. The Partnership page runs in it as well: five territories stand one after another on paper, every one visible at once, each a large rounded photo that blooms from navy monochrome into full colour as it scrolls in, beside the territory's name and a two-row ledger of what the partner brings and what Ciright brings. The Company page runs in it as well: it tells the company through its press record alone, as press stories on paper (the outlet's photograph, the headline, an excerpt, and the source set below the headline), opened by the real podium photograph and broken once by a full-bleed navy Portal chapter whose stories run as legs, one per city: a lead story beside its photograph, then a grid of the rest, every outlet photograph in its own colour. The Contact page runs in it as well, as "The Night Desk": one rounded white room outlined by a hairline on the paper, set in ink, holds the whole form, written as a letter ("Hi Ciright, I'm [name] from [company]."), with the visitor's details as underlined blanks inside running sentences that grow as they are typed, the subjects as the site's own pills on a row of their own, and a message box to close it, followed on paper by the headquarters address beside a street map drawn from OpenStreetMap data in the site's own palette.

Density is low and confident. One statement per section, set large, no scaffolding around it: no eyebrow labels, no icon grids, no metric tile walls beyond the single hero stats strip. Motion is scroll-led and physical (smooth inertial scroll, cards that recede as the next one stacks over them, product family spreads that build themselves as they arrive, evidence tiles that arrive scattered and settle into a straight, level row by the time the row reaches mid-screen, and the founding statement and CEO quote that ink in word by word as they are read, under a rule that draws itself from the left), with a blend-mode cursor and magnetic pills as the studio signature. The brands reel adds one more: a row of posters that holds still while vertical scroll walks it sideways, the poster at the focus point fully lit and blooming into its photograph's own colour while its neighbours fall back to navy monochrome, dim and settle back by distance. The partnership page adds two scrubbed moments: territory photos that bloom into colour as they rise into view, and a four-verb headline that lights up word by word. The company page adds one: a night photograph revealed through a circle that widens from the photographed Portal ring to the full frame as it scrolls in, blooming from navy to colour as it opens. The contact page adds two small ones: the Send pill's label rolls up to name the subject just picked, and the map pin's ring opens once when the map scrolls into view. Every motion collapses to a still, fully legible page under reduced motion.

The anti-reference is the old ciright.com: enterprise image sliders, stock globes, and icon-feature grids. The signature that replaces them is media set *inside* type: a rounded video-still pill living in a headline word.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-white paper ground, ink type, and night-indigo fields as the only saturated surface.
- One variable grotesk (Geologica) across every role; hierarchy comes from size, weight 350 to 500, and tight negative tracking.
- Full pills for every action; circular arrow chips on text links and rows.
- Rounded media set into headlines as the recurring signature.
- Products listed as named type in hairline rows or text grids inside a night field, never as icon tiles.
- Brands shown as tall full-bleed photo posters (a name at the top, a tagline and copy pinned to the foot, the photograph filling everything behind them), navy monochrome until focus blooms them to colour, never as a logo grid.
- Partnership territories shown all at once as full sections (photo beside name and a You bring / Ciright brings ledger), never behind tabs.
- Press coverage shown as stories (outlet photograph, headline, excerpt, then a source line under the headline), or inside the Portal field as a lead story over a grid of story cards, never as a press-logo wall or a grid of clipping scans.
- Scroll-scrubbed depth (stacking, tilt, zoom-out) instead of static shadows.
- One form, written as a letter inside a white room with ink type: blanks set in the sentence's own type that grow with what is typed, the site's own ink-outlined pills for subjects, a dark red error tone as the letter's only hue, and a status line that says exactly what happened.
- Places drawn from street data in the site palette (lavender ground, mint parks, navy water, white roads), never embedded or photographed.
- Everything scales from one comp unit, so the page is a proportional scale model of the 1280px comp.

## Colors

A monochrome paper-and-ink page punctuated by a single deep navy family anchored on #01042E, with two pale pastels reserved for evidence tiles. (The night family was indigo until it was rebased on the brand navy; the "indigo" wording elsewhere in this file refers to this family.)

### Primary
- **Ciright Navy** (night-1, #01042E): the brand color. The ground every night field falls to at its edges, and the solid accent fill: text selection, caret, scrollbar thumb, and the arrow chip inside the cursor tag. On brand posters it is the flat card ground, the dimming veil over posters that are out of focus (up to 50% opacity), and the tint of the two legibility scrims over each photo: 74% at the top edge fading out by 38% down under the name, and 96% at the foot holding 78% to 36% up before fading out by 68%. On partnership territory photos it is the ground behind the image and the tint of a single foot scrim (92% at the bottom edge, 55% at 30% up, gone by 58%) under the proof overlay. On the Company page it is the flat ground of the full-bleed Portal chapter (the page's one night field) and the placeholder behind the podium frame and every press photograph on paper.
- **Lifted Navy** (night-3): the same hue raised so it reads as color against ink. Glow center of every night field, the hover fill that sweeps up through black pills, the quote mark, and the "to" figure in the case-study headline. It is also the thumb of the thin scrollbar under the unstaged brands reel, and the centre of the Portal chapter's glow.
- **Deep Navy** (night-2): the mid-field of the solution-card radial and the floor of the trust-band gradient. In the Portal chapter it is the glow's mid-stop (Lifted Navy at the centre, Deep Navy at 22vw, gone by 46vw).

### Secondary
- **Twin Blue** (twin): the "digital twin light." Keyboard focus rings, footer link hover, and the low glow under the closing headline. It is light, never a surface.
- **Ring Light** (ring): the pale periwinkle of the radar rings, always at partial alpha and only inside night fields: 6 to 12% in the concentric rings (including the three around the Portal chapter's step frame), 35% on product-tag outlines, 70% on a product row's rule when it is hovered. Like twin blue, it is light, never a fill.

### Tertiary
- **Survey Mint** (mint) and **Blueprint Lavender** (lavender): the case-study result tiles, alternating. Lavender doubles as the hover sweep inside white pills. Nowhere else, except the Contact page's HQ map, where lavender is the land (and the halo behind every map label) and mint is the parks.

### Alert
- **Signal Red** (alert): the validation tone inside the Contact desk, and the only hue in the letter: an invalid blank's or message box's rule, its focus line, the error list under the reach line and the error line under the message box (both 15px). Declared on the desk itself, not on the page root, and dark enough to read as 15px text on the desk's white (about 5.5:1).

### Contact desk and map tones
The desk is a white room set in ink: neutral greys and alpha steps of ink, each with one job. Inside the desk, moonlight is overridden to Ink and haze to Graphite, so the letter reads like the paper pages around it.
- **Hint** (hint): a neutral grey for the placeholder hints inside the letter's blanks and message box ("your name", "you@company.com"), about 4.9:1 on white. Declared on the letter, not the page root. On keyboard focus the hint inside a blank fades further, mixed 55% toward white.
- **Coal** (coal): the sweep inside a checked (ink) subject pill, one step up from Ink.
- **Desk rule** (desk-rule): ink at 22%, the resting 2px underline of every blank and of the message box. It measures about 1.7:1 against white, so at rest a blank is identified by its hint text as much as by its rule. **Desk rule, hover** (desk-rule-hover): ink at 50%, the same rule under the pointer; focus turns it solid ink.
- **Map rail** (map-rail): ink at 30%, the dashed railway lines on the HQ map.
- **Pin wash** (pin-wash): Ciright Navy at 8%, the fill inside the HQ map pin's ring.
- White (white) is the Contact desk's ground and its autofill mask, the label of a checked subject pill and of the Send pill, the text of a desk selection, every road on the map, the pin dot's 6-unit stroke, and the ink chip's arrow.

### Neutral
- **Paper** (ground): page ground, header glass tint, mobile menu, scrollbar track.
- **Ink** (ink): all type on paper and in the Contact desk (every letter line, typed answers, the status line), the desk's caret, focus rule, focus line, text selection, subject-pill border and focus outline, and checked subject pill, black pills, the stats rule, the component-row hover wipe, the brands reel's progress fill, and the outlet name in a press source line.
- **Graphite** (ink-soft): body copy inside tiles, the case-study paragraph, the body of the paper end card in the brands reel, the two engine-column paragraphs on the partnership page, and the excerpt of a press story.
- **Pencil** (muted): secondary copy on paper (component descriptions, quote attribution, struck-through "from" figure, the "/ 08" total beside the brands reel counter, the "You bring" / "Ciright brings" row labels and the engine-parts lead on the partnership page, the date and medium in a press source line, and the podium caption).
- **Hairline** (hairline): 1px dividers on paper (component list, header rule once scrolled, the top rule of each partnership territory, its ledger rows, the territories engine row and the engine columns, and the top rule of an image-less press story), the inset outline of the brands reel's end card, and the resting track of the reel's progress line.
- **Night Black** (night-black): the brands capsule top, the trust-band gradient start, and the closing night block.
- **Void** (night-0): placeholder behind media in night fields (including behind each brand poster's full-bleed photo, the Portal step frame and every Portal leg photograph), and the lowest black in pill media.
- **Moonlight** (on-night) and **Haze** (on-night-soft): primary and secondary type on indigo. Haze is a night-only tone; on paper it falls below legible contrast. In the Portal chapter, moonlight sets place names, story titles and outlet names, and haze sets the lede, each leg's date line, the lead story's excerpt and each story's date. Inside the Contact desk neither appears: moonlight is overridden to Ink and haze to Graphite.
- **Night Hairline** (night-hairline): moonlight at 16%, in the Portal chapter, the top rule of each leg head and of each leg's story grid, and on mobile the rule under every story row.
- **Photo Tint** (photo-tint): the color-blend layer that recolors grayscale photographs into the indigo world (every brand poster, every partnership territory photo, and in the Portal chapter the step-through photograph). On posters its opacity follows focus, full when out of focus and 15% when lit; on territory photos and the Portal photo it follows the same opacity formula as each photo lights. Used only inside the photo treatment.

### Named Rules
**The Night Field Rule.** Saturated color appears only as a night field (a radial or vertical blend of night-1, night-2, night-3, starting from night-black where the field meets a black section). Paper sections stay monochrome; indigo never appears on paper except as a single emphasized word or figure. The HQ map is drawn material, not a field: its navy water and navy pin are map ink on a lavender ground. The Contact desk is not a night field either: it is a white room on paper with no glow.

**The Indigo Darkroom Rule.** Photographs that move (brand posters, partnership territories, the Portal step-through) enter a night field through the darkroom: grayscale with raised contrast and reduced brightness under a photo-tint color blend. Static photographs, meaning the homepage solution-card media and the Products suite family frames, keep their own colour and are never tinted. Rendered product imagery enters untreated, except on a brand poster, where a render takes the same treatment as the photos around it. The two exceptions bloom by the same formula: the brand poster in focus, and a partnership territory photo once it has scrolled into view, each returns to (nearly) its image's own colour (see The Focus-Light Rule). Every other poster, and every photo elsewhere, stays tinted. Inside the Company page's Portal field the step-through night photograph blooms by its own brighter ramp as it opens (see The Step-Through Rule). Outlet press photographs are the one standing exception: on paper and on the Portal legs alike they enter in their own colour (see The Outlet Colour Rule).

**The Outlet Colour Rule.** An outlet's press photograph is evidence, and it always shows in its own colour, on paper and inside a night field alike. It is never tinted, veiled or bloomed; hover answers only with a zoom.

**The Evidence Pastel Rule.** Mint and lavender mark proof (results), alternating tile by tile. They are not decoration and not section backgrounds. The HQ map is the one other place they appear, as land and parks.

**The Street-Data Map Rule.** A place is shown as a map drawn from OpenStreetMap data in the site palette: lavender land, mint parks, Ciright Navy water, white roads in three weights, a dashed ink-at-30% rail, and one navy ring-pin. ### Named Rules
**The Sentence-Is-The-Heading Rule.** Section headings are statements or plain titles. Never precede them with a small uppercase kicker; the uppercase label role exists only to caption data.

**The Tight-At-Size Rule.** Tracking tightens as size grows: -0.04em at display and headline, -0.02 to -0.035em for statement, index and title, normal for body. Never track display type positive.

**The Type-At-The-Edges Rule.** Type on a photo poster lives only where its scrims are: the name at the top, and the figure, tagline, body and link in the foot. The open middle belongs to the photograph. Name, tagline and figure are white; the body stays haze. A territory photo carries type only in its foot scrim: the white "Built here" line over a haze proof line, with the arrow chip in the opposite corner.

**The Source-Below Rule.** A press story names its source after the headline: outlet (weight 500), date, optional medium, in a small line under the title and excerpt. The outlet is never set above the headline as a label, and never as a logo.

**The Halo Rule.** A label on the map sits on a lavender halo (a stroke painted under the fill: 8 units behind street labels, 10 behind the pin label; 12 and 14 on mobile) so it reads over the roads. The river label is the one exception: moonlight set inside the navy water with no halo, turned along the river's course and flipped so it never reads upside down.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat at rest and gets its depth from scroll, blend and layering rather than shadows. Homepage solution cards stack physically: while the next card travels from 85% to 15% of the viewport, the one beneath scales to 0.94 and dims to 60% brightness. This happens only while cards are actually sticky. Media zooms out from 1.15 to 1 as it enters, and solution-card photos drift ±6% for parallax. Each Products family spread builds itself once as it arrives (the words of its name rise, its statement row fades up, its product hairlines draw), and its media band opens with scroll from a pill into a rounded frame, as the homepage hero's twin pill does. Indigo fields are lit from within by radials, and faint concentric rings sit over the solution cards like a radar sweep. The sticky header is frosted paper (88% ground, 14px blur, 1.4 saturation) and only gains a hairline once scrolled.

The Brands reel gets depth from light rather than stacking, in a treatment called the focus bloom. Each poster carries a lit value from 0 to 1 that sets everything together. The card scales from 0.95 at rest to 1 when lit, anchored at the bottom centre so the row keeps one floor line, and a Ciright Navy veil covers it at 50% when unlit and not at all when lit. Behind the type, the photo's filter moves from grayscale 1, contrast 1.22 and brightness 0.46 when unlit to grayscale 0.1, contrast 1.12 and brightness 0.76 when lit. Its Photo Tint color blend falls from full opacity to 15%, so an out-of-focus poster is navy monochrome and the poster in focus blooms to its true colour. The photo also settles from 1.14 to 1.04 scale as it takes focus. Lit falls off with distance from the focus point, measured in poster steps: fully lit within 0.2 of a step, fully dark beyond 0.65, with a smoothstep between. Unstaged, every poster is fully lit, so the native swipe row shows every photo in colour. On a hover-capable pointer, hovering a poster eases its photo in a further 5% (0.9s ease-out); hover changes only that zoom, never the light.

Partnership territory photos use the same bloom, called the scroll bloom here, with one input instead of distance: each photo's lit value is scrubbed from 0 to 1 as it travels from its top at 85% of the viewport to its centre at 55% (0.5s scrub smoothing). The filter, Photo Tint opacity and image scale (1.14 to 1.04) follow the poster formula exactly; there is no navy veil and the frame itself never scales. Scrolling back drains it again. Without motion the lit value rests at 1, so every territory photo shows in colour.

The Company page's Portal chapter gets depth from a reveal, called the step-through. Its field is lit by one glow (Lifted Navy to Deep Navy, gone by 46vw) and three ring-light rings (12%, 9% and 6%; 60, 90 and 124vw across), all centred on the point where the photographed Portal ring sits inside the step frame, so the rings read as the Portal's own echo. ### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Tile lift** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px -30px rgba(1, 4, 46, 0.35)`): the only drop shadow in the system, under the mint and lavender result tiles, tinted indigo so it reads as a cast shadow of the night palette.
- **Header rule** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgba(10, 11, 14, 0.14)`): a hairline, not elevation; appears after 40px of scroll.
- **End-card outline** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(10, 11, 14, 0.14)`): an inset hairline, not elevation; it draws the paper end card of the brands reel so it keeps the poster silhouette without a fill.
- **Blank focus line** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 0` Ink, or `0 2px 0` Signal Red on an invalid blank or message box): a second 2px line under the focused rule, so the underline reads 4px while focused. A rule, not elevation.
- **Autofill mask** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 100px #fff inset`, with the fill text forced to ink): keeps browser autofill from painting a pale box inside a blank. Not elevation.

### Named Rules
**The Scroll-Is-Depth Rule.** Depth is shown by motion (stacking, receding, zooming, tilting) and by light inside night fields. Do not add drop shadows to cards, pills or media.

**The Room-To-Pin Rule.** Pinning needs room and motion. Homepage solution cards pin at every width but sit in flow under reduced motion, and they recede only while actually sticky. The Brands reel pins only at 901px wide and 640px tall or more, under the same motion conditions; elsewhere it is a native swipe row.

**The Focus-Light Rule.** In a walked reel, focus is shown by light, colour and scale, driven by scroll position alone: the poster at the focus point is fully lit and its photo blooms to true colour, while its neighbours drain to navy monochrome, dim under a navy veil and settle back to 0.95. A lit poster, and a partnership territory photo that has scrolled into view, are the only places a darkroom photograph returns to its own colour, and both reach it through the same filter and tint formula. Hover never lights a poster or a territory photo, and nothing dims or drains when motion is off. The Company page's Portal field adds one more bloom, the step-through photograph, which blooms by scroll through its own night ramp; its leg photographs are outlet evidence in their own colour and never light or drain.

**The Step-Through Rule.** A reveal centres on what the photograph shows: the Portal chapter's clip circle, glow and rings all share the photographed Portal ring as their centre, and the circle opens from the ring's own size. It is the Company page's one authored motion moment, scrubbed by scroll and fully open when motion is off.

- **The letter:** "Hi Ciright, I'm [name] from [company]." then "I'd like to talk about" with the subject pills on a row of their own, then "You can reach me at [email] or on [phone].", then "Here is what I have in mind:" over the message box. All four blanks are required; the subject is optional.
- **Subject pills:** a wrapping row of six pills: General (the default) and the five real subjects (Centili SIM SECURE, Channel partners, M&A, Sansar and spatial computing, Media relations). Each is the shared pill markup, so it matches every pill on the site: 49 units tall (at least 44px), 22-unit padding, weight 400, the rising ellipse sweep, and magnetic under a fine pointer. At rest it is outlined in 1px ink with an ink label, the same outline as the Products and Partnership jump pills; on a hover-capable pointer an ink sweep rises through it and the label turns white, as on the Products family pills. Checked, it fills ink with a white label and its sweep turns Coal. Colour and fill change over 0.3s. Keyboard focus raises the sweep and draws a 2px ink outline offset 3px around the pill. Picking one is optional.
- **Named send** (the page's authored motion): the Send pill's label names the chosen subject ("Send message" for General, otherwise "Send my M&A inquiry", "Send my Sansar inquiry" and so on) and, on every change, the new label rolls up into place from 80% below, unblurring from 3px and fading in (0.6s ease-out). While sending it rolls to "Sending…". Under reduced motion the label simply changes.
- **Status line:** a polite live region beside the Send pill, in ink, that reports what happened in one or two sentences:
  - **Invalid:** a submit with problems moves focus to the first invalid field and the status announces the count ("One field needs attention." or "3 fields need attention.").
  - **Sending:** the form is marked busy, fields and pills are disabled, and the request gives up after 15 seconds.
  - **Error:** "Your message did not send, so Ciright has not received it." The details stay in the form, with a link to contactus.ciright.com as the fallback.
  - **Unconnected:** when no submission endpoint is configured, the desk says the form is not connected yet and nothing was sent, keeps the details, and points to contactus.ciright.com. It never shows the sent panel.
  - Editing any field after an error or unconnected message clears it.
- **Sent panel:** only after the server accepts the message, the form is replaced by "Thanks, {first name}." at headline size, a graphite line confirming the message was sent and naming the reply address, and a primary "Send another message" pill that clears the form. The panel takes keyboard focus when it appears.

**The Honest Send Rule.** The desk shows exactly what happened and nothing more. Success appears only when the server has accepted the message; every failure says that nothing was received, keeps what the visitor typed, and offers the old contact form. The desk never invents a response time, routing promise, email or phone number.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** size new elements in comp units (`calc(N * var(--u))`) so they scale with the rest of the page.
- **Do** put every action in a full pill, and end every card-level "explore" link with a trailing circular arrow chip.
- **Do** let paper sections alternate with night fields; open each section with its statement or plain title as the h2.
- **Do** run moving photographs (posters, territories, the Portal photo) through the indigo darkroom (grayscale plus photo-tint color blend). Static solution-card and suite photos keep their own colour. The other exceptions are a lit brand poster and a scrolled-in partnership territory photo, whose bloom returns the photo to true colour, and an outlet's press photograph, which always keeps its own colour.
- **Do** credit every shipped photo (title, author, licence, and "tinted and cropped" when treated) in that page's row on the credits page.
- **Do** hang statements and index text from the 376-unit label column.
- **Do** tie every scroll effect to scroll position (scrubbed, or triggered once at a scroll threshold like a family spread's entrance) and give it a still fallback: under reduced motion (or `?still`), smooth scroll, reveals, scrubs and the cursor are off, sticky cards become static, and family spreads show fully built.
- **Do** keep twin blue as light: focus rings, link hovers, glows.
- **Do** list products as named type in a full-width ledger (name 7fr, text 5fr, hairline rows that fill Ink on hover) inside their family spread on paper.
- **Do** register a scroll destination for any jump target that lives inside a pinned room (a brand poster maps to the point that centres it); otherwise point the jump at the element itself with a scroll-margin-top that clears the header, as each family spread does.
- **Do** build every page from the shared pill, header, closing, footer, and cursor, and mark the current page with `aria-current="page"`.
- **Do** build a horizontal reel as a native scroll-snap row first, and stage it (pin, walk, focus light, counter) only at 901px wide and 640px tall or more; register every staged poster as a scroll destination and walk the page to a poster when keyboard focus enters it.
- **Do** reuse the shared rolling counter (window, reel, total) for any position count; the total takes haze inside a night field and pencil on paper.
- **Do** show all five partnership territories at once as full sections with alternating photo sides, and distinguish Ciright's side of a ledger by weight (450), not colour.
- **Do** reuse the poster bloom formula unchanged for any photo that blooms to colour on scroll, and let it rest fully lit when motion is off. The one sanctioned variant is a night photograph, which may ramp brightness to 1.08 (0.46 + 0.62 × lit) as the Portal step-through does.
- **Do** show press coverage as stories: the outlet's photograph (32-unit radius), the headline, an excerpt, then the source line (outlet at 500, date, optional medium) under the headline. Inside a night field, give each leg a lead story (16:10 photograph beside title, haze excerpt, source and explore link) over a four-column grid of 3:2 story cards, every photograph in its own colour; mirror the lead on alternate legs, and turn the grid into thumbnail rows on mobile.
- **Do** centre a reveal, its glow and its rings on the subject the photograph shows, as the Portal step-through centres on the photographed ring.
- **Do** keep section titles on mobile below the hero headline size (9vw clamped 2 to 2.4rem).
- **Do** point every Contact and demo action at `/contact` in the same tab; open a new tab only for links that leave the site.
- **Do** write a form as a letter inside a white room on paper (1px hairline border, section radius, ink type): blanks in the sentence's own type (46 units clamped 28 to 54px) that grow with their content over a 2px ink-at-22% rule, visually hidden labels, Hint-coloured placeholders, an ink rule plus a 2px ink focus line on focus, Signal Red on error (a list under the line, a line under the message box), and autofill masked back to white.
- **Do** build choice controls from the shared pill markup (49 units, weight 400, the ellipse sweep) rather than a restyled one-off pill.
- **Do** say exactly what a submission did: focus the first invalid field and announce the count, say nothing was received on failure or when the form is unconnected, keep the visitor's details, and show the sent panel (and move focus to it) only after the server accepts.
- **Do** let a choice name the action: the Send label rolls to the chosen subject, and simply changes under reduced motion.
- **Do** draw places from OpenStreetMap data in the site palette (lavender land, mint parks, navy water, white non-scaling roads, dashed rail, one ring-pin), halo map labels in lavender, and credit the data under the ODbL on the credits page.

### Don't:
- **Don't** reintroduce the old ciright.com vocabulary: enterprise image sliders, stock globes, or icon-feature grids.
- **Don't** put small uppercase kickers or eyebrows above headings; the uppercase label style captions data only.
- **Don't** build metric tile grids. The hero stats strip (figures over a 2px ink rule) is the one place numbers are listed; the only other figure is a single proof number set in the foot of one brand poster.
- **Don't** add drop shadows to pills, cards or media; the tile lift on result tiles is the only shadow.
- **Don't** place full-color photography on indigo (a lit brand poster, a bloomed territory photo, the opened Portal step-through photo, or an outlet's press photograph on a Portal leg excepted), or indigo fields on paper as decoration.
- **Don't** tint, veil or bloom an outlet's press photograph; hover answers only with a zoom.
- **Don't** set a press outlet above a headline as a label, or show coverage as a press-logo wall or a grid of clipping scans.
- **Don't** put a cursor word ("Read", "Watch") on a story's text; it belongs on the photograph or thumbnail only.
- **Don't** put the partnership territories behind tabs, a switcher or a pinned stage; the user rejected the tabbed territory map.
- **Don't** use mint or lavender outside evidence tiles, the white-pill hover sweep, and the HQ map's land and parks.
- **Don't** box form fields or set them as a grid of labels over inputs, fake a successful send, or promise a response time, routing, email or phone number the business has not given.
- **Don't** use Signal Red outside the Contact desk or as a fill; it is the desk's error tone only.
- **Don't** bring navy, a glow or rings into the Contact desk's letter, or give its subject pills a navy sweep or a twin-blue ring; the letter is ink on white.
- **Don't** embed a map widget or use a screenshot or stock office photo for a location; draw it.
- **Don't** give products icons, logo tiles, or colored badges; the outline product tag is the only marker.
- **Don't** show brands as a logo grid, or put a logo, icon or placeholder graphic on a poster; every brand poster carries one real photograph or render full bleed behind its name.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface or icon font; icons are inline stroked SVG at 1.6 to 1.8 stroke with round caps.
